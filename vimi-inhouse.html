Common questions

What in-house teams ask us.

We already have an agency managing our tracking. Why do we need Atlas?
Agencies set up tracking. Atlas monitors whether it stays correct. These are different jobs. Your agency configures GTM — Atlas validates whether what GTM is sending to Google and Meta is actually right, continuously. We work alongside your agency, not instead of them.
Our GA4 looks fine. How do we know there's a problem?
GA4 showing data doesn't mean the data is correct. Duplicate events, misfired parameters, and missing values all show up as normal traffic in GA4 — they just silently corrupt the signals going back to your ad platforms. Atlas validates what's actually being sent, not just whether something is being sent.
Can't we just do a one-time audit?
You can — and that's exactly what the diagnostic is. But signals break continuously with every site update, new campaign, and CMS change. A one-time audit is accurate for about 2–4 weeks. The managed retainer keeps the signal chain healthy permanently.
How much technical involvement is required from our team?
Minimal. The initial diagnostic requires read access to your GTM and ad platform accounts. After that, Atlas runs in the background. Your team gets plain-language alerts when something needs attention — no dashboards to check, no training required.
How is this different from ObservePoint or Trackingplan?
Those tools scan for tag presence. Atlas validates the full conversion signal chain end-to-end — from data layer to platform API — and is delivered as a managed service. You don't need to learn or operate another tool.
