Common questions

What agencies ask us.

We already have GTM set up for every client. Why do we need this?
GTM being "set up" doesn't mean it's sending correct signals. Most implementations drift within weeks of any site change. Atlas validates what GTM is actually sending vs. what Google and Meta need to receive — continuously, not just at setup.
How is this different from a one-time GTM audit?
A one-time audit is accurate for about 2–4 weeks. Every site update, CMS change, and new campaign can break signals. Atlas monitors continuously — the diagnostic is your entry point, not the full engagement.
Will our clients know we're using Atlas?
No. All client-facing reports are white-labelled to your agency. Atlas runs in the background — your clients see your brand, not ours.
How is this different from ObservePoint or Trackingplan?
Those tools scan for tag presence. Atlas validates the full conversion signal chain end-to-end — from data layer to platform API — and is delivered as a managed service. You don't operate it yourself.
Can we start with just one client?
That's exactly how we recommend starting. Run the diagnostic on your most important client. See what's broken. Then decide whether to roll it out across your book. No commitment beyond the initial diagnostic.
