The problem

Your bidding AI is learning
from bad data. Every day.

When conversion signals are wrong, the ad platforms don't stop optimising. They optimise harder — towards the wrong outcomes. Smart Bidding targets worse audiences. CAPI sends duplicate events. Attribution reports tell a story that isn't true.

01
Duplicate & misfired events

Every duplicate conversion event trains Google and Meta's algorithms to believe a certain action is more common than it is — skewing audience targeting, bidding, and ROAS reporting.

Silent damage
02
Tags break with every site change

A CMS update, new landing page, or checkout redesign can silently break conversion tracking. You won't know until campaign performance drops and the client asks why.

Ongoing risk
03
Nobody owns the signal layer

Marketing assumes dev handled it. Dev assumes the agency set it up. Nobody is continuously verifying that what's being sent to Google and Meta is actually correct.

Accountability gap
04
Attribution is a best guess

If events are duplicating, misfiring, or missing parameters, every attribution report is wrong — and you're making budget decisions based on it every single month.
